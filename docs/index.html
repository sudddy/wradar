              Privacy

              Your resume never leaves your browser. Its text, your name and your employers are never kept. So jobs stay scored next time, this device keeps only a label such as "PDF resume", your skills, how each one shows on your resume (latest job, older work or listed), its years of use and months since you last used it, and your total years, level, role and degree. Skills come from a fixed list, so a company named like one, such as Stripe, can show up as a skill.

              Your Saved, Applied and Hidden jobs stay on this device too: the mark, when you made it, and the job's title, company, link and location. A hidden job also keeps the mark it had before, so showing it again brings that back. So does each job you open from the list or Your best matches, marked Clicked: just its company and title, and the date, for the latest 2,000. So do your filter choices.

              None of this is sent anywhere. Remove your resume any time from Your resume. Clearing site data removes everything.

              Run your own copy

              The code is on GitHub. Fork sudddy/wradar, edit config.json to change companies, locations and rules, and GitHub Actions keeps your copy fresh.
